I am currently a research assistant professor at the Department of Computing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). Before joining PolyU, I was a post-doctoral fellow at the Department of Computer Science, the University of Hong Kong. I received my Ph.D. degree from PolyU in 2020 under the supervision of Prof. Man Ho Allen Au.

I am interested in:

  • Lattice-Based Cryptography

  • Zero-Knowledge Proofs

  • Blockchain Technology
